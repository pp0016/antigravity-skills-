# Demonetization Defense Strategy

YouTube heavily flags "Inauthentic Content" and "Mass Produced Content" for AI automation channels. 

When generating a channel setup, you MUST enforce the following guidelines to ensure the channel can be monetized.

## The Risk Factors (What gets you demonetized)
- Template-based automation (same intro, same music, same pacing in every video).
- "Zero Human Transformation" (just throwing a script into an AI video generator without edits).
- Synthetic, robotic-sounding voiceovers without human emotion or pacing.

## The Defense Mechanisms (What you must recommend)

1. **The "Sutradhar / A-Roll" Method:** 
   The user should shoot a 5-10 second clip of themselves (can wear a mask or hide identity partially) acting as the narrator or "Sutradhar" introducing the story or concluding the moral. This proves human presence.

2. **Visual Variation:**
   Do not rely on static AI slideshows. The videos must have dynamic pacing. Use Veo 3.1 or Omni for "short film" style visuals where characters talk (if advanced), or highly varied B-roll with strong editing (if standard narrator style).

3. **Original Sound Design:**
   Do not use the same 3 royalty-free tracks for every video. Build custom soundscapes using tools like Lyria 3. Human effort is heavily judged by audio engineering and sound design.

4. **The "BTS" Strategy:**
   Recommend uploading one "Behind the Scenes" video showing the prompt engineering, editing timeline, and research process. This serves as insurance against manual YouTube reviews.
