# vidIQ Workflow Templates — Niche-Agnostic

Pre-built workflow templates for every YouTube content creation scenario.
Each template includes exact tool call sequences, credit costs, and expected outputs.

Replace `[NICHE]`, `[TOPIC]`, `[CHANNEL]`, `[VIDEO_URL]` with your actual values.

---

## 1. 🚀 New Channel Launch Research (~35 credits)

**When to use**: Starting a new channel, entering a new niche, or pivoting your content strategy.

### Step-by-Step

| Step | Tool | Cost | Action |
|------|------|------|--------|
| 1 | `credits_balance` | 0 | Verify you have ≥35 credits |
| 2 | `trend_categories` | 0 | Browse all available categories, identify your [NICHE] |
| 3 | `keyword_research` | 5 | Research primary keyword for [NICHE] — get search volume & competition |
| 4 | `keyword_research` | 5 | Research secondary keyword variation |
| 5 | `keyword_research` | 5 | Research third keyword angle (long-tail) |
| 6 | `outliers` | 5 | Find outlier videos in [NICHE] from small channels |
| 7 | `channel_analytics` | 5 | Analyze top competitor #1 in [NICHE] |
| 8 | `channel_analytics` | 5 | Analyze top competitor #2 in [NICHE] |
| 9 | `generate_titles` | 5 | Generate first 5-10 video ideas based on gaps found |
| **Total** | | **35** | |

### Expected Output
A **Channel Launch Report** containing:
- Niche viability score (search volume vs competition)
- 3 keyword clusters to target
- 2 competitor breakdowns with content gaps
- Outlier videos proving the niche has viral potential
- 10 video ideas ranked by opportunity score

## Custom Workflow Builder

If none of the above templates fit, build a custom workflow:

1. Start with `credits_balance` (always free)
2. Use `trend_categories` (free) to orient
3. Pick 2-4 standard tools based on the specific need
4. Never use heavy tools (10 credits) unless the user specifically wants deep video analysis
5. Always total up the cost and confirm with the user before executing

### Budget Allocation by Creator Stage

| Stage | Recommended Monthly Allocation |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| **Pre-launch** (no channel yet) | 1x New Channel Launch (35) + 2x SEO Optimizer (20) + Daily routines (0) = ~55 credits |
| **Early growth** (0-1K subs) | 2x Weekly Planning (50) + 2x SEO Optimizer (20) + 1x Competitor Dive (20) = ~90 credits |
| **Growth phase** (1K-10K subs) | 4x Weekly Planning (100) + 1x Competitor Dive (20) + 1x Deep Analysis (15) = ~135 credits |
| **Scaling** (10K+ subs) | Full budget across all workflows, consider upgrading to Boost plan |
